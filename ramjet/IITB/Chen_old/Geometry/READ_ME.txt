The Geometry file contains the geometry of the cd nozzle created using various softwares.

chen1.geo (2D) is the model used for meshing and simulations.

chen3d.geo is 3-dimensional form of the geometry.

chen_trip.geo (2D) has a trip on its upper wall as mentioned in the paper.

chen_trip_3d.geo (3D) is a complete replica of the experimental setup.
